# San Jose Permit Navigator]: AI for Confusing permit information

Client: City of San José, Small-business permitting
Team: Ishaan Pabla, Elias Duong, Jovin Duong, Joe Garcia, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I helped build and test the Replit demo, refining the user-input flow and Permit Action Plan screens.

## The problem
First-time business owners must navigate Planning, Building, Fire, and Environmental Services to determine which permits apply to their project, leading to confusion and incomplete applications

## What we built
- A prototype where users enter their address, business category, modifications, project description, and preferred language.
- An AI-generated Permit Action Plan that explains requirements, documents, departments, sources, and next steps in plain language, along with a records page for saved applications.
- The AI only creates a draft recommendation; authorized City staff must review, edit, approve, reject, or override the information.

## What I would do next
One gap is that the prototype does not yet have reliable role-based authentication or live City data. I would build secure resident and City staff accounts, connect the system to verified City sources, and test the workflow with business owners and permit reviewers.
